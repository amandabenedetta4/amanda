Genre-Specific Marketing Playbooks

Decade-Long Expertise Across Every Major Commercial Publishing Category

Genre Mastery Is Not Optional. It Is Essential.

Book marketing is not a one-size-fits-all discipline. The strategies that produce explosive results in contemporary romance will fail completely if applied to epic fantasy. The advertising audiences that drive thriller sales have virtually no overlap with the audiences that purchase self-help non-fiction. The blurb structures that convert romance readers are fundamentally different from the blurb structures that convert business book readers.

Over the past decade, I have managed campaigns across virtually every major commercial genre. This breadth of experience allows me to deploy genre-specific strategies that are precisely calibrated to the expectations, behaviors, and purchasing patterns of your target readers.

Subgenres Covered: Contemporary Romance, Dark Romance, Romantic Suspense, Paranormal Romance, Fantasy Romance (Romantasy), Historical Romance, Romantic Comedy, Small Town Romance, Billionaire Romance, Sports Romance, MC Romance, Reverse Harem

Commercial Romance: The Highest-Volume Genre in Independent Publishing Demands the Most Sophisticated Marketing Infrastructure

Romance is the single largest genre in the independent publishing marketplace, representing approximately 40 to 50% of all Kindle Unlimited page reads. This massive volume creates enormous opportunity but also intense competition. Standing out in the romance market requires a marketing approach that is specifically calibrated to the unique behaviors and expectations of romance readers.

Tropes-First Marketing

Romance readers search for books by trope as much as by genre. Your marketing assets must clearly and immediately signal the specific tropes your book delivers. This includes incorporating trope language into your blurb, your ad copy, your keyword targeting, and your A+ Content. Common high-performing tropes include enemies to lovers, fake dating, grumpy sunshine, second chance, forced proximity, and found family. I ensure every marketing touchpoint speaks your readers' trope language fluently.

Visual Meta Ad Creative Optimization

Romance advertising on Meta platforms is uniquely visual. The most effective romance ads feature character-focused imagery, emotional tension cues, and trope-signaling visual elements. I develop ad creative that immediately communicates the emotional experience your book delivers, stopping scrolling readers in their tracks and compelling them to click through to your product page.

Kindle Unlimited Page Read Maximization

The majority of romance readers consume books through Kindle Unlimited. Your marketing strategy must be optimized to maximize KENP page reads by driving high-volume traffic to KU-enrolled titles and ensuring that your book's opening chapters are compelling enough to prevent early abandonment. I engineer campaigns specifically designed to generate and sustain high daily page-read counts.

Rapid Series Read-Through Pipelines

Romance readers are voracious series consumers. The most profitable romance marketing strategy focuses on acquiring readers at Book 1 and maximizing read-through across the entire series. I engineer aggressive loss-leader campaigns on Book 1 combined with optimized back-matter links and series page configurations to drive maximum read-through velocity from first page to final book.

High-Converting Reader Magnets

The most effective reader magnets for romance authors are bonus epilogues, exclusive novellas featuring secondary characters, and alternate point-of-view chapters. These assets convert at significantly higher rates than generic lead magnets because they deliver additional emotional content that romance readers crave. I design magnets that feel like gifts rather than marketing tools.

Subgenres Covered: Psychological Thriller, Domestic Suspense, Police Procedural, Legal Thriller, Medical Thriller, Cozy Mystery, Hardboiled Detective, Espionage, True Crime Narrative, Historical Mystery

Thriller, Mystery, Crime, and Psychological Suspense: High-Tension Hook Development and Comp-Author Targeting for the Most Competitive Fiction Categories

Thriller and mystery readers are driven by plot tension, pacing, and the promise of a satisfying resolution. Your marketing must immediately communicate the stakes, the mystery, and the urgency that will compel readers to purchase your book. These readers make fast purchasing decisions based on the intensity of your hook and the credibility of your comparable author positioning.

High-Tension Hook Development

Thriller blurbs and ad copy must create immediate psychological tension. The most effective hooks present a compelling scenario with high stakes and an unresolved question that the reader feels compelled to answer. I craft hooks that trigger the "need to know" response that drives thriller purchases, using proven tension-building frameworks refined across dozens of thriller campaigns.

Comp-Author Advertising Targeting

Thriller readers are highly loyal to specific authors. Targeting fans of established thriller authors such as James Patterson, Lee Child, Karin Slaughter, Gillian Flynn, and John Grisham through Amazon product targeting and BookBub comp-author campaigns is one of the most effective acquisition strategies in this genre. I identify the precise comp authors whose readership overlaps with your ideal audience.

BookBub Ad Stacking

BookBub is disproportionately effective for thriller and mystery genres because the BookBub subscriber base skews heavily toward these categories. I deploy aggressive BookBub advertising campaigns timed to coincide with discounted pricing windows and new release launches, maximizing the conversion potential of this uniquely powerful promotional platform.

International Multi-Retailer Wide Scale

Thriller and mystery titles perform exceptionally well in international markets, particularly the UK, Australia, and Canada. A wide distribution strategy that includes targeted international advertising campaigns can significantly expand your reader base and revenue streams while taking advantage of lower competition and advertising costs in non-US markets.

Subgenres Covered: Epic Fantasy, Urban Fantasy, Dark Fantasy, Space Opera, Military Science Fiction, Cyberpunk, Dystopian, LitRPG, Progression Fantasy, Grimdark, Sword and Sorcery

Science Fiction, Epic Fantasy, and LitRPG: Worldbuilding-Driven Marketing for the Most Dedicated and High-Spending Reader Communities

Science fiction and fantasy readers are among the most dedicated, high-spending, and community-oriented readers in the publishing marketplace. They consume long series, purchase special editions, and actively participate in genre-specific communities. Marketing to this audience requires authenticity, genre fluency, and an understanding of the unique economics of long-form series publishing.

Lore-Accurate Worldbuilding Hooks

SFF readers are repelled by generic marketing copy. Your blurbs, ad copy, and reader magnets must demonstrate genuine fluency in the conventions and expectations of your specific subgenre. I craft marketing copy that speaks the language of your target readers without resorting to cliches or generic fantasy tropes, ensuring authenticity that builds trust with discerning genre readers.

Box Set and Bundle Packaging

SFF readers prefer to consume complete series. Packaging your series into comprehensive box sets creates high-ticket product offerings that appeal to binge readers and generate significant revenue per transaction. I design box set strategies that maximize both discovery and per-transaction value across KU and direct purchase channels.

Direct-to-Consumer Special Edition Campaigns

The SFF reader community is uniquely willing to purchase premium special editions, signed copies, and exclusive merchandise through direct-to-consumer channels. I develop D2C strategies including Kickstarter, BackerKit, and Shopify campaigns that capitalize on this willingness to maximize per-reader revenue far beyond what retail platforms offer.

Community Onboarding Sequences

SFF readers value community and engagement. Your email welcome sequences should introduce new readers to the world of your series, provide exclusive lore content, and invite them into your inner circle of dedicated fans. I design onboarding experiences that transform casual readers into passionate advocates who anticipate every new release.

Subgenres Covered: Business and Entrepreneurship, Personal Finance, Self-Help, Health and Wellness, Memoir and Autobiography, True Crime, History, Science and Technology, Relationships, Spirituality, Leadership, Productivity

Non-Fiction, Business, Memoir, and Personal Development: Authority Positioning and Lead Generation Ecosystems for Maximum Commercial and Professional Impact

Non-fiction marketing operates on fundamentally different principles than fiction marketing. Non-fiction readers are searching for solutions to specific problems, answers to specific questions, or expertise in specific domains. Your marketing must position you as the authoritative source of the knowledge or transformation your book promises.

Authority Positioning and Credibility Architecture

Your marketing assets must establish your credentials, expertise, and unique perspective immediately. This includes strategic use of professional credentials, media appearances, speaking engagements, and industry recognition in your blurb, A+ Content, and advertising copy. I position your book as the definitive resource in its category through carefully crafted authority signals.

Lead Generation Ecosystems

Non-fiction books are most valuable when they serve as the entry point to a broader business ecosystem. I design lead generation funnels that capture reader email addresses and nurture them toward higher-value offerings such as courses, coaching programs, consulting engagements, or speaking opportunities. Your book becomes the top of a profitable conversion funnel.

Amazon Bestseller Category Targeting

Achieving bestseller status in specific Amazon categories provides significant credibility and visibility for non-fiction authors. I strategically select and target categories where bestseller status is achievable with realistic sales volumes, maximizing the perceived authority of your book and creating a powerful credential for your professional practice.

High-Ticket Backend Alignment

For non-fiction authors who offer high-ticket services or products, your book marketing strategy should be aligned with your backend revenue model. I design campaigns that optimize for lead quality and backend conversion rather than just book sales volume, ensuring that every reader acquired has the potential to become a high-value client or customer.

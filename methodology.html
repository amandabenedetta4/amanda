Methodology

The Proprietary 5-Phase Methodology

A Battle-Tested Framework Developed Over 10+ Years and 100+ Campaigns

A Systematic Approach to Publishing Marketing Success

After more than a decade of managing book marketing campaigns across every major genre and retail platform, I have developed a proprietary five-phase methodology that consistently produces predictable, repeatable, and scalable results. This framework is not theoretical. It has been refined through hundreds of real-world campaigns, thousands of hours of data analysis, and millions of dollars in managed advertising spend.

Every engagement I undertake follows this structured methodology, adapted and customized to the specific requirements of your catalog, genre, and commercial objectives. The five phases are sequential and cumulative, meaning each phase builds upon the foundation established by the previous one.

01
Deep-Dive Diagnostic and Catalog Audit
Duration: Typically 5 to 10 business days

Every successful marketing campaign begins with a comprehensive understanding of the current state of your publishing business. Before I recommend a single tactic or strategy, I conduct an exhaustive audit of your entire catalog, your existing marketing infrastructure, your competitive landscape, and your historical performance data. This diagnostic phase is the foundation upon which every subsequent decision is built.

Sales Data Analysis

Review of your historical sales data across all retail platforms. This includes unit sales trends, revenue patterns, seasonal fluctuations, and platform-specific performance breakdowns. I analyze your sales data to identify patterns, anomalies, and opportunities that are not visible at the surface level. This data forms the quantitative foundation for all strategic recommendations.

Advertising Account Review

If you have existing advertising accounts on Amazon, Meta, or BookBub, I conduct a thorough review of your campaign structures, keyword targeting, audience configurations, bid strategies, and historical performance metrics. This review identifies wasted spend, missed opportunities, and structural inefficiencies that can be immediately corrected for improved performance.

Conversion Rate Assessment

Analysis of your current product page conversion rates across all retail platforms. This includes evaluation of your blurbs, covers, A+ Content, pricing, review counts, and star ratings. I identify the specific elements that are suppressing your conversion rates and prioritize them for optimization based on expected impact and implementation complexity.

Competitive Landscape Mapping

Identification and analysis of the top 20 to 50 competing titles in your specific genre and subgenre. This includes analysis of their pricing, cover design, blurb strategies, review volumes, advertising presence, and promotional activity. Understanding your competitive landscape is essential for positioning your books effectively and identifying gaps you can exploit.

Reader Funnel Evaluation

Assessment of your existing reader acquisition infrastructure including your email list size, growth rate, engagement metrics, lead magnet effectiveness, and automated sequence performance. If you do not yet have a reader funnel, this component identifies the highest-priority opportunities for building one from scratch.

Phase 1 Deliverable

A comprehensive written diagnostic report delivered via email. This report includes all findings, data analysis, competitive benchmarks, and a prioritized list of recommended actions organized by expected impact and implementation complexity. This document becomes the roadmap for every decision made in subsequent phases.

02
Strategic Blueprint and Funnel Architecture
Duration: Typically 7 to 14 business days

Based on the findings from the Phase 1 diagnostic, I develop a comprehensive strategic blueprint that serves as the master plan for your entire campaign. This blueprint is a detailed, written document that outlines every strategic decision, tactical deployment, and expected outcome for the duration of our engagement. Nothing is left to improvisation or guesswork.

Campaign Objective Definition

Clear articulation of your primary and secondary campaign objectives. These objectives are specific, measurable, and time-bound. Examples include achieving a specific BSR threshold within the first week of launch, reducing cost-per-acquisition by 30% within 90 days, or increasing series read-through from Book 1 to Book 2 by 25%.

Budget Allocation Strategy

Detailed breakdown of how your advertising and promotional budget will be allocated across platforms, campaign types, and time periods. This includes specific daily and monthly spend recommendations, bid ceiling parameters, and budget scaling triggers based on performance thresholds that determine when to invest more aggressively.

Timeline and Milestone Mapping

A comprehensive campaign timeline that maps every major action, activation, and milestone from the start of the engagement through the conclusion of the campaign. This timeline ensures that both you and I have complete clarity on what is happening and when throughout the entire project lifecycle.

Contingency Planning

Pre-defined contingency protocols for common campaign scenarios including underperforming launches, unexpected competitive activity, algorithm changes, and budget constraints. Having contingency plans in place ensures that we can respond quickly and effectively to any situation without losing momentum or wasting resources.

03
Asset Production and Conversion Optimization
Duration: Typically 10 to 21 business days

With the strategic blueprint approved, Phase 3 focuses on the production and optimization of every marketing asset required for campaign execution. This is the most labor-intensive phase of the methodology, and it is where the quality of execution separates exceptional campaigns from mediocre ones. Every asset is built to convert, tested for effectiveness, and refined before deployment.

Blurb and Hook Copywriting

Writing or rewriting your book descriptions using the psychological copywriting frameworks described in the Services section. Every blurb is crafted to maximize conversion rates by following the proven Hook, Conflict, Stakes, and Call to Action sequence that has been tested across hundreds of campaigns and thousands of variations.

Advertising Creative Development

Creation of all advertising creative assets including ad copy variations, headline tests, image compositions, and video scripts. Multiple creative variations are produced for every campaign to enable rigorous A/B testing during the execution phase. Each variation targets a different emotional trigger or reader motivation.

Keyword and Metadata Harvesting

Comprehensive keyword research and metadata optimization across all retail platforms. This includes harvesting high-volume, low-competition keyword phrases, optimizing backend search fields, and selecting the most advantageous category placements based on current market data and competitive analysis.

Email Funnel Construction

Building and configuring your reader magnet delivery system, automated welcome sequences, and nurture email flows. Every email is written, formatted, and tested before being activated. All deliverability settings are configured to maximize inbox placement rates and minimize spam folder delivery.

04
Market Deployment and Launch Execution
Duration: Varies based on campaign type (typically 30 to 90 days)

Phase 4 is where the strategic planning and asset production of the previous three phases converge into live market execution. Every element of your campaign is deployed according to the precise timeline established in the strategic blueprint. This phase requires constant attention, real-time decision making, and rapid optimization to maximize results during the critical execution window.

Staggered Advertising Deployment

Paid advertising campaigns are launched in a carefully sequenced order. Amazon campaigns are typically activated first to build keyword relevance and organic ranking. Meta campaigns follow to drive external traffic and trigger Amazon's algorithmic preference for externally sourced conversions. BookBub campaigns are timed to coincide with promotional pricing windows for maximum impact.

Promotional Activation Sequencing

Newsletter features, cross-promotions, and discount stacking activations are deployed in a precise sequence designed to create cumulative sales velocity. Each promotional activation is timed to build upon the momentum generated by the previous one, creating a compounding effect that maximizes algorithmic recognition.

Real-Time Performance Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of all campaign metrics including impressions, clicks, conversions, cost-per-acquisition, and Return on Ad Spend. Performance data is analyzed daily to identify trends, anomalies, and optimization opportunities that require immediate action to capture or correct.

Dynamic Campaign Adjustment

Real-time adjustments to bids, budgets, targeting parameters, and creative assets based on live performance data. Underperforming elements are paused or revised immediately. High-performing elements are scaled aggressively to capture maximum value during their peak performance window.

05
Data Analysis, Scaling, and Evergreen Systems
Duration: Ongoing (typically 30 to 90 days post-launch, or indefinite for retainer engagements)

The final phase of the methodology focuses on analyzing the complete campaign data, identifying scaling opportunities, and transitioning your marketing infrastructure from launch mode to evergreen mode. This is where the long-term value of the campaign is realized and where the foundation for sustained, predictable monthly revenue is established.

Comprehensive Campaign Post-Mortem

A detailed written analysis of the entire campaign including total spend, total revenue, Return on Ad Spend, cost-per-acquisition, conversion rates, and all other key performance indicators. This post-mortem identifies what worked, what did not work, and what should be done differently in future campaigns. Every insight is documented for future reference.

Profitable Campaign Scaling

Identification and aggressive scaling of the highest-performing advertising campaigns, keywords, and audience segments. Campaigns that have demonstrated consistent profitability are scaled to higher daily budgets to maximize revenue generation while maintaining positive ROAS thresholds.

Evergreen System Implementation

Transitioning your marketing infrastructure from launch-specific campaigns to ongoing evergreen systems that generate consistent sales without requiring continuous active management. This includes automated advertising campaigns, ongoing promotional calendars, and self-sustaining reader funnel systems that work for you around the clock.

Backlist Integration

Integrating the momentum generated by your frontlist launch into your broader backlist catalog. This includes deploying series read-through campaigns, cross-selling promotions, and backlist advertising to ensure that the investment in your launch produces compound returns across your entire catalog for months and years to come.

Services

Full-Service Book Marketing Suites

Comprehensive, Data-Driven Marketing Solutions for Every Stage of Your Publishing Career

A Complete Ecosystem of Publishing Marketing Services

Every service I offer is designed to function as part of an integrated, full-funnel marketing ecosystem. While each suite can be engaged independently based on your specific needs, the most powerful results come from combining multiple services into a cohesive campaign architecture that addresses every stage of the reader journey from initial discovery through long-term catalog loyalty.

Below you will find detailed descriptions of all six core service suites. Each suite includes a comprehensive breakdown of deliverables, strategic objectives, and expected outcomes. All services are custom-scoped to your specific genre, catalog size, release schedule, and commercial objectives.

01
Tier-1 Launch Architecture and Chart Acceleration
Engineered Launch Campaigns That Force Retail Algorithms to Promote Your Book

A successful book launch is not a single event. It is a carefully orchestrated sequence of strategic actions deployed across a 90-day window before, during, and after your release date. The goal is to generate enough concentrated sales velocity in a compressed timeframe to trigger the retail algorithms that control visibility, recommendation placement, and organic discovery.

Most authors approach their launches with a scattered collection of tactics: a few newsletter swaps, a random promo blast, and some hopeful social media posts. This approach produces inconsistent, unpredictable results at best. My launch architecture methodology is fundamentally different. Every element of your campaign is strategically sequenced to build cumulative momentum that peaks on release day and sustains through the critical post-launch period.

90-Day Pre-Launch Strategic Blueprint

A comprehensive, day-by-day campaign timeline covering the entire pre-launch window. This document outlines every action item, deadline, promotional activation, and advertising deployment from 90 days before release through 30 days post-release. You will know exactly what is happening, when it is happening, and why it is happening at every stage of the campaign.

Pre-Order Strategy and Algorithmic Seeding

Strategic deployment of pre-order campaigns designed to accumulate sales velocity before your release date. Pre-orders are one of the most powerful tools available for launch momentum because they concentrate sales into a single reporting period on release day. I will structure your pre-order timeline, pricing, and promotional activations to maximize this effect.

ARC Team Assembly and Review Acquisition

Building and managing a high-quality Advance Reader Copy team tailored to your specific genre and target audience. This includes identifying qualified reviewers, distributing advance copies through compliant channels, tracking review submission rates, and ensuring that verified reviews appear on your product page within the critical first 72 hours of release. All ARC activities are conducted in full compliance with retailer terms of service.

Promotional Stacking and Velocity Engineering

Coordinating a strategic sequence of promotional activations across premium newsletter networks, cross-author distribution lists, and discount stacking platforms. The goal is to layer multiple promotional events in rapid succession to create an overwhelming surge of sales velocity that triggers algorithmic recommendation engines including BookBub, Freebooksy, RobinReads, Bargain Booksy, and Fussy Librarian.

Release Week Algorithmic Trigger Activation

Deploying a concentrated burst of conversions during the first 48 to 72 hours of release to activate Amazon's "Also Bought" recommendation engine, "Popularity List" placement, and automated email recommendation systems. These algorithmic triggers are the key to sustained organic visibility beyond the initial launch window.

Post-Launch Cliff Mitigation (Weeks 2 through 8)

Most books experience a dramatic sales decline after the first week. My post-launch stabilization strategy deploys secondary advertising scaling, strategic price pulses, and targeted promotional activations to sustain momentum through the critical weeks 2 through 8, ensuring that your book maintains visibility and sales velocity well beyond the initial launch spike.

02
Paid Advertising Architecture and Scaling
Data-Driven Ad Campaigns Focused Strictly on Profitable Return on Ad Spend

Paid advertising is the single most scalable reader acquisition channel available to authors in 2026. However, the majority of authors who attempt to manage their own ad campaigns end up wasting significant portions of their budgets on poorly targeted keywords, ineffective creative assets, and misconfigured campaign structures. My advertising management service eliminates this waste by deploying precision-targeted, continuously optimized campaigns across all major retail and social advertising platforms.

Amazon Advertising (AMS) Full Campaign Management

Complete setup, management, and optimization of Amazon Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored Display campaigns. This includes precision keyword harvesting using exact, phrase, and broad match types. Continuous negative keyword scrubbing to eliminate wasted spend on irrelevant search queries. Direct ASIN product targeting to position your book alongside competing and complementary titles. Category placement optimization and ongoing bid management based on real-time performance data.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Conversion Campaign Management

Full-funnel Meta advertising campaigns designed to drive direct sales to your retail listings. This includes creative ad hook generation and copywriting tailored to your genre's reader psychology. Dynamic image and video ad creative development. Deep demographic, interest-based, and Lookalike audience modeling based on your ideal reader profile. Conversion tracking and attribution setup. Continuous A/B testing of headlines, images, copy, and audience segments.

BookBub Ads Campaign Strategy and Management

Strategic deployment of BookBub advertising campaigns targeting comp-author audiences and genre-specific reader segments. This includes precision bid optimization for high-click-through-rate auction wins. Comp-author targeting campaigns for launch windows, discounted box sets, and wide-retailer pushes. Ongoing performance monitoring and bid adjustment to maximize cost efficiency across all campaign types.

Cross-Platform Attribution and ROAS Reporting

Comprehensive written reports delivered via email detailing campaign performance across all platforms. Reports include total spend, impressions, clicks, click-through rates, cost-per-click, conversions, cost-per-acquisition, and overall Return on Ad Spend (ROAS). Every report includes strategic recommendations for the next optimization cycle to ensure continuous improvement.

Read-Through Profitability Modeling

Advanced calculation of your series read-through economics to determine exact cost-per-acquisition targets. This ensures that your advertising spend on Book 1 is profitable when accounting for the downstream sales of Books 2, 3, 4, and beyond. Most authors only measure ad profitability on the single title being advertised. I measure profitability across your entire catalog for maximum long-term revenue optimization.

Ad Creative Development and Testing

Creation of multiple ad creative variations including compelling headlines, emotionally resonant copy, genre-appropriate imagery, and conversion-focused calls to action. Each creative variation is systematically tested through controlled A/B experiments to identify the highest-performing combinations. Winning creatives are scaled aggressively while underperformers are replaced with new variations.

03
Storefront and Conversion Rate Optimization
Transforming Your Book's Product Page Into a High-Converting Sales Machine

Driving traffic to your book's retail listing is only half the equation. If your product page fails to convert browsers into buyers, every dollar you spend on advertising and promotion is wasted. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) is the process of systematically improving every element of your book's retail presence to maximize the percentage of visitors who click the "Buy Now" button.

Psychological Blurb and Hook Copywriting

Complete re-engineering of your book description using proven commercial copywriting frameworks. Your blurb is the single most important conversion element on your product page. I write blurbs that follow the precise psychological sequence that compels readers to purchase: The Hook (an irresistible opening line that creates instant curiosity), The Conflict (the central tension that makes the reader need to know what happens), The Stakes (the consequences that raise emotional investment), and The Call to Action (the closing trigger that converts interest into purchase).

Amazon A+ Content Strategy and Design Direction

Strategic planning and creative direction for premium Amazon A+ Content modules. This includes editorial layout design, comparative series grids, character highlight panels, quote callouts, and visual banner compositions optimized for mobile shoppers. A+ Content has been shown to increase conversion rates by 5 to 15% when properly executed with compelling visuals and strategic copy placement.

Precision Metadata and Category Architecture

Advanced keyword research and backend metadata optimization using current 2026 retail search algorithms. This includes identification of high-volume, low-competition keyword phrases. Strategic BISAC category selection to position your book in categories where bestseller status is achievable with realistic sales velocity. Backend keyword field optimization across all seven Amazon keyword slots for maximum discoverability.

Cover and Packaging Diagnostic Audit

Objective, data-driven analysis of your book cover's commercial viability. This includes thumbnail readability testing at the size most shoppers actually see your book. Genre signaling analysis to ensure your cover immediately communicates the correct genre and subgenre to target readers. Typography evaluation for legibility and market alignment. Competitive positioning analysis against the top 20 bestselling covers in your specific category.

Pricing Strategy and Elasticity Analysis

Data-backed pricing recommendations based on genre benchmarks, competitor analysis, and your specific catalog economics. Pricing is one of the most powerful conversion levers available, and even small adjustments can produce significant changes in sales volume and overall revenue. I analyze the optimal price point for maximum royalty yield based on your distribution model and reader expectations.

Back-Matter Optimization and Series Architecture

Strategic optimization of your book's back matter including calls to action, series page links, reader magnet offers, newsletter signup prompts, and next-book teasers. The back matter of your book is prime real estate for converting a single-book reader into a catalog-wide fan. I engineer every element of your back matter to maximize read-through, email capture, and long-term reader retention.

04
Reader Funnels and Direct-to-Consumer Infrastructure
Building a Proprietary Reader Database That You Own 100%

The most valuable asset in your publishing business is not your book. It is your reader database. Retail platforms own the customer data from every sale they process. You do not have access to the email addresses of the readers who purchase your books on Amazon, Apple, or Kobo. This means that every time you launch a new book, you are forced to pay advertising platforms to reacquire readers you have already earned. The solution is to build your own direct-to-reader communication channel that you control completely.

Reader Magnet Strategy and Content Engineering

Development of high-converting lead generation assets designed to capture qualified reader email addresses. This includes strategic planning for bonus epilogues, exclusive prequel novellas, starter library collections, deleted scene compilations, and character backstory packages. The reader magnet must be compelling enough that readers willingly exchange their email address to receive it. I design magnets calibrated to your specific genre's reader psychology.

Automated Welcome and Nurture Email Sequences

Creation of 5-to-7 part automated email onboarding flows that activate immediately when a new reader subscribes. These sequences are designed to build author-reader intimacy, introduce your backlist catalog, deliver exclusive content, and drive immediate and ongoing sales. Every email in the sequence is strategically crafted to move the reader closer to becoming a lifelong fan who purchases every title you release.

Newsletter Health and Deliverability Optimization

Comprehensive audit and optimization of your existing email list to maximize inbox placement rates. This includes list cleaning to remove inactive and invalid addresses. Spam score reduction through content and formatting optimization. Domain authentication setup including DKIM, DMARC, and SPF records. Click-to-open rate (CTOR) optimization through subject line testing and content structure improvements.

Direct-to-Consumer Store Strategy

Planning and optimization of direct book sales channels through platforms such as Shopify, WooCommerce, or Ream. This includes strategy for special edition releases, signed copy sales, bundle packages, early access pre-orders, and exclusive merchandise offerings. Direct-to-consumer sales allow you to retain 90 to 95% of revenue compared to the 35 to 70% retained through retail platforms.

Subscriber Segmentation and Behavioral Targeting

Implementation of advanced subscriber segmentation based on reader behavior, genre preferences, purchase history, and engagement levels. This allows you to send targeted, relevant emails to specific reader groups rather than generic blasts to your entire list. Segmented campaigns consistently outperform broadcast emails in open rates, click rates, and conversion rates by significant margins.

Launch Email Sequence Engineering

Design and implementation of dedicated launch email sequences that leverage your existing subscriber base for maximum release-day impact. These sequences include pre-launch anticipation builders, release-day urgency triggers, post-launch social proof amplifiers, and follow-up sequences that capture readers who missed the initial announcement. Your email list becomes your most powerful and cost-free launch weapon.

05
Backlist Catalog Revival and Series Read-Through
Transforming Dormant Backlist Titles Into Consistent Monthly Revenue Streams

Your backlist is your most underutilized asset. Many authors focus exclusively on their newest release while allowing their older titles to slowly decline into obscurity. This is a massive missed opportunity. A properly optimized backlist catalog can generate more consistent monthly revenue than your frontlist titles, and it does so without requiring the intensive launch investment of a new release. Every book you have ever published has the potential to earn meaningful revenue for years to come if properly positioned and promoted.

Comprehensive Catalog Diagnostic Audit

A thorough analysis of your entire published catalog including historical sales trends, advertising performance, conversion rates, blurb effectiveness, cover competitiveness, and competitive positioning. This audit identifies exactly where revenue is leaking and which titles have the highest potential for revival. Every finding is documented in a comprehensive written report delivered via email.

Series Read-Through Amortization Strategy

Structuring strategic "loss leader" campaigns on Book 1 of your series to fuel high-margin sales on subsequent titles. This includes permafree and $0.99 promotional pricing strategies, Kindle Unlimited enrollment optimization, and targeted advertising campaigns designed specifically to maximize the number of readers who continue from Book 1 through the entire series to the final book.

Box Set and Bundle Packaging Strategy

Strategic repackaging of your series into multi-book box sets for Amazon Kindle Unlimited, audio bundles, and multi-retailer high-ticket offerings. Box sets create new product listings that capture additional search visibility and appeal to binge readers who prefer to purchase complete series at once. I design box set strategies that maximize both KU page-read revenue and direct purchase revenue.

Evergreen Promotion Calendar

Development of a rolling 12-month promotional calendar that keeps your backlist titles active and visible throughout the year. This includes scheduled discounted promotions, BookBub Featured Deal applications, newsletter feature stacking, and strategic ad pulse campaigns timed to coincide with seasonal reading trends and genre-specific demand cycles for maximum impact.

Cover Refresh and Repackaging Consultation

Objective assessment of whether your backlist covers are suppressing sales due to outdated design trends, poor genre signaling, or thumbnail readability issues. If a cover refresh is recommended, I provide detailed creative direction including typography specifications, color palette guidance, imagery requirements, and competitive market references to ensure your refreshed covers meet current genre standards.

Audiobook and Multi-Format Integration

Strategic planning for expanding your backlist into audio, hardcover, and large print formats to capture additional market segments and revenue streams. This includes narrator selection guidance, ACX versus wide audio distribution analysis, and marketing strategies specific to audiobook discovery and promotion across Audible, Chirp, Libro.fm, and library lending platforms.

06
Multi-Retailer Wide Distribution Strategy
Expanding Your Reach Beyond Amazon Into Global Retail Markets

While Amazon dominates the ebook market, relying exclusively on a single retail platform creates significant business risk. Algorithm changes, account issues, and policy shifts can devastate an author's income overnight. A wide distribution strategy diversifies your revenue streams and protects your business against platform-specific disruptions while opening up entirely new reader markets that your competitors are ignoring.

Multi-Platform Launch Coordination

Strategic launch planning and execution across Apple Books, Kobo Writing Life, Barnes and Noble Press, Google Play Books, and Smashwords or Draft2Digital. Each platform has unique algorithmic behaviors, promotional opportunities, and reader demographics that require tailored approaches. I develop platform-specific strategies that maximize visibility and sales on each retailer independently.

Global Library Distribution Optimization

Metadata and pricing optimization for library lending platforms including OverDrive, Libby, BorrowBox, and Hoopla. Library distribution represents a significant and often overlooked revenue stream that also serves as a powerful reader discovery channel. Readers who discover your books through libraries frequently become direct purchasers of your future titles and backlist catalog.

Geo-Targeted International Campaigns

Strategic advertising and promotional campaigns targeting international markets including the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, and other high-volume English-language territories. International markets often have lower advertising costs and less competition than the US market, making them highly efficient targets for reader acquisition and revenue diversification.

Platform-Specific Merchandising and Promotion

Leveraging retailer-specific promotional opportunities including Apple Books editorial features, Kobo promotional placement applications, Barnes and Noble storefront visibility programs, and Google Play promotional pricing tools. Each platform offers unique merchandising opportunities that most authors are unaware of or fail to take advantage of effectively.

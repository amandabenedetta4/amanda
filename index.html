About Amanda

Turning Stories Into Success

With 12 years of experience in book marketing, Amanda Benedetta has mastered the art and science of connecting compelling stories with their perfect readers. Her data-driven approach combines traditional publishing wisdom with cutting-edge digital marketing strategies to create launches that don't just make noise; they make sales.

Amanda's methodology centers on understanding each book's unique DNA and crafting bespoke campaigns that resonate authentically with target audiences. From debut authors to established names, she's helped countless writers break through the noise of a crowded marketplace and claim their spot on bestseller lists.

Her strategic approach has earned her a reputation for turning overlooked manuscripts into must-read phenomena, leveraging everything from Amazon algorithm optimization to influencer partnerships and social media virality.

★★★★☆ 4.3/5 2,071 ratings
Young Adult Contemporary Romance Book 1 of 3: Dimple and Rishi

When Dimple Met Rishi

by Sandhya Menon

The Challenge:

Breaking into the competitive YA contemporary market with a diverse romance featuring Indian-American protagonists, a demographic traditionally underrepresented in mainstream publishing.

The Strategy:

Amanda developed a multi-layered campaign that positioned the book as both a heartwarming romance and an important #OwnVoices narrative. The strategy included targeted outreach to diversity-focused book bloggers, strategic use of early reader reviews, and a social media campaign highlighting the book's authentic cultural representation.

The Results:

  • ⭐ 4.3/5 stars with over 2,071 ratings
  • 📚 Became the first book in a successful three-book series
  • 🎯 Secured prominent placement in Amazon's Teen & Young Adult categories
  • 💬 Generated significant buzz in the #DiverseBooks community
  • 🎬 Film rights optioned
"

Amanda understood exactly how to position my debut novel in a crowded market. Her strategic approach to reaching readers who were hungry for diverse love stories was instrumental in 'When Dimple Met Rishi' finding its audience. She didn't just market a book; she helped launch my entire career as an author. Her insights into both the commercial and cultural aspects of book marketing are unmatched.

Sandhya Menon, Author

Amanda doesn't just follow trends; she anticipates them.

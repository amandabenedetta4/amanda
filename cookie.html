Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies allow websites to recognize your device and store information about your preferences or past actions, making your browsing experience more convenient and personalized.

2. How We Use Cookies

Amanda Benedetta Book Marketing Services uses cookies to:

  • Enable essential website functionality
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you interact with our website
  • Improve our website performance and user experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Deliver relevant marketing and advertising content
  • Protect against fraud and enhance security

5. How to Control Cookies

5.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Only accept first-party cookies
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser
  • Notify you when a cookie is being set

Note: Blocking or deleting cookies may impact your experience on our website and limit the functionality available to you.

5.2 Browser-Specific Instructions

Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

5.3 Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of targeted advertising cookies through these services:

  • Google Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Facebook Ad Preferences: https://www.facebook.com/ads/preferences
  • Network Advertising Initiative: https://optout.networkadvertising.org
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: https://optout.aboutads.info

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to have your online activity tracked. Currently, there is no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals.

At this time, our website does not respond to DNT browser signals or mechanisms. We will continue to monitor developments around DNT browser technology.

7. Cookies and Mobile Devices

If you access our website through a mobile device, cookies and similar technologies work in much the same way as on desktop computers. You can typically manage cookies through your mobile browser settings.

Additionally, you can control advertising identifiers on mobile devices:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Advertising > Limit Ad Tracking
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

8. Cookie Lifespan

Cookies have different lifespans:

8.1 Session Cookies

These are temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. They help our website remember what you choose when you move from page to page during a single browsing session.

8.2 Persistent Cookies

These cookies remain on your device for a set period (specified in the cookie) or until you delete them. They help our website recognize you when you return and remember your preferences.
